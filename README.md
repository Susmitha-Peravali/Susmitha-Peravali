## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate who likes finishing what I start — not just prototyping, but actually shipping working, deployed systems. Most recently that meant building **Sendora**, a full-stack email scheduler with BullMQ-driven delayed jobs, Redis-backed atomic rate limiting, idempotent job processing, and crash-recovery reconciliation — then taking it all the way through a real production deployment (Vercel + Render + Postgres + Redis), not just `localhost`.

- 🔭 Currently building **Sendora** — a production-grade email scheduling platform
- 🌱 Deepening my systems/backend engineering skills — queues, concurrency, distributed job processing
- 💬 Ask me about BullMQ, Redis, REST API design, or React/TypeScript
- 🏆 200+ DSA problems solved on LeetCode · Top 12 Finalist, Mission Schrödinger Cat Hackathon (National, 200+ participants)
- 📫 Reach me at **teja.peravali0@gmail.com**
